# AccountPacks

The product bundles one neutral technical sample in accountpacks/neutral/neutral.json. Family examples demonstrate bounded technical contracts. Put customer-authored JSON in work/accountpacks; code changes are not required. The neutral sample supplies no proprietary creative methodology. The sample procedure has status `TODO`, meaning no complete method is supplied. Define procedures for your own content in your AccountPack and keep appropriate source and rights records. A procedure status does not certify the quality or commercial suitability of your output.
